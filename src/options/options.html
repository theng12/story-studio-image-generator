All video models cost credits. Image-to-video: animates the scene's image with its motion prompt. Saves the clip to “11 Video Assets” or Downloads.

“Existing” uploads {scene_id}.png from Story Studio. “Generate” makes a new image (from the visual prompt) then animates it.
