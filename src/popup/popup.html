Pollinations options
Runs in the background — no tab needed. Free/anonymous is rate-limited (~1 image/15s) and watermarked; register at auth.pollinations.ai and paste a token to remove the watermark and go faster. Aspect ratio follows your scene setting.
Grok Imagine options
Set model, aspect ratio, style and Image/Video on the Grok Imagine page — Grok saves them to your account and the extension replays your prompt with whatever you last chose there. Set them once; they apply to every scene. (Grok takes no per-request options, so there's nothing to set here.)
Google AI Studio options
Open aistudio.google.com in image mode (pick an image model, e.g. Nano Banana 2 Lite) and keep that tab visible. The extension types “Generate an image of …”, runs it, and reads the result. Model / aspect ratio / resolution are whatever you set in the AI Studio tab. Uses your free daily image quota; when it's exhausted the queue pauses until you press Resume after the reset.
Qwen Studio options
Uses your signed-in chat.qwen.ai session and asks Qwen to create one image per scene. The extension reinforces this ratio with both exact dimensions and natural-language canvas instructions, but it remains best-effort because Qwen may choose its default shape. Human Pace applies between every request. Qwen uses one submission per scene; the Flow reCAPTCHA attempt setting does not apply. Explicit quota/rate-limit messages pause the queue until you press Resume.
Reference-photo attachment is not enabled yet: Qwen currently treats uploads as conversation/editing context, and that workflow still needs a safe consistency test.
